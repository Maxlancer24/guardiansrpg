Economy & Progression

Two currencies, a card collection, achievements that pay, and seasons that take nothing away from you.

Currencies

Oil CoinsOIL
The main currency. Earned from most modes, daily message activity, and events. Spent on potions, crafting, enhancement, the roulette, founding a clan (12,500) and clan upgrades.
War Oil
Secondary currency from large-scale combat — Castle Siege and War. Used for siege and war progression.

Codex

A collection of cards you unlock as you play — enemies, gear, regions, allies and artifacts. Each has a discovery tier and a deeper tactical note you only unlock by fighting the thing enough. It is both a reference and a completion goal, and it pays XP and Oil as you fill it.

Badges

Achievements for hitting milestones — crit counts, wins, and other tracked stats. Some grant small combat bonuses such as crit or dodge chance.

Seasons and medals

When a season changes, the server takes a snapshot and awards medals for the level milestones you crossed.

Level reachedMedal
10Bronze
20Silver
30Gold
40Diamond

A season change does not reset your character. Nothing is wiped, nothing is taken. It records where you finished and rewards the milestones you crossed. View a season card with !profile @user s1.

Bonds

Bonds track relationships between players. Playing with — or against — the same people builds a shared history. Check yours with !bonds, notable ones with !bonds_top, and the log with !bonds_history.

Bonds are also a currency. At the Weapon Forge, 100 Bonds permanently unlocks either of two weapon recipes — Revenant Edge and Requiem Aegis — both built around a fallen ally's spirit. Unlock once, then craft and enhance them like any other weapon.
