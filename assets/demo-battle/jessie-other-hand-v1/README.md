segmentation, recoloring or redrawing is used in these production assets.

Uniform aspect ratio, 500px silhouette height and baseline y=525.
Generated using output/jessie-other-hand-v1/prepare-original.py in the bot workspace.

Normal attacks alternate A/B; the same two fire times and damage callbacks
are used. B muzzle is registered at (447,111) on the aimed drawing.
