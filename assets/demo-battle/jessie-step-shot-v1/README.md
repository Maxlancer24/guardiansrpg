# Jessie — double step study

`atlas.webp`: 16 chronological transparent frames, 512 × 512 cells, four columns.
`sequence.json`: timing, ground anchor and muzzle positions used by the hidden animation lab.

Artwork generated with ImageGen using the approved Jessie artwork as reference. Prompt summary: preserve Jessie's face, brown ponytail, navy-and-gold coat with burgundy lining, black outfit, boots, turquoise charm and twin silver revolvers; draw a coherent 4 × 4 sequence of preparation, step, slight turn, two shots and recovery; transparent background, consistent proportions, no text or baked-in effects. Frames were mechanically extracted and aligned at a common scale and ground baseline.

This is a prototype sprite animation, not a finished cinematic. The previous articulated idle and single shot are retained for comparison. No account, combat or database state is affected.
