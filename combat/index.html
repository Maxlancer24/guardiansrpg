Combat

Base RPG formulas and a stat calculator. Learn how damage, healing, Parry, critical chance and dodge are calculated.

For actions, tactics and worked examples, read Combat, step by step. This page explains the base numbers.

These are base RPG rules, using your effective combat stats. Equipment, enhancements, mastery, clan bonuses, territories, NPC passives and mode effects can change the result. Numbers in the battle log can also reflect rounding.

STR strengthens attacks and your guard/counter. CON gives more maximum HP, Rest healing and defense. AGI affects damage, initiative, critical chance, dodge and mitigation. Critical chance and dodge chance are different curves.

Show the probability formulas

Through AGI 10: critical chance = AGI%; dodge = floor(AGI ÷ 2)%. Above 10: critical = [10 + floor((AGI − 10) ÷ 3)]%; dodge = [5 + floor((AGI − 10) ÷ 5)]%. Add applicable bonuses; probability is capped at 100%. Floor means discard the decimal part.

For a normal hit, dodge can avoid the hit before damage is calculated. If it connects, offensive multipliers are calculated; Parry compares against that damage or Rest adds its vulnerability multiplier. Applicable AGI mitigation, damage reduction, shields and special effects then affect how much HP is actually lost. An offensive damage number is not a promise of that much HP loss.
