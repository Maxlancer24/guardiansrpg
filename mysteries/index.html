The world

Mysteries

Valestia keeps secrets. Some of them are waiting for a player who does the right thing in the right place — and no two are alike.

There are secrets in here

Scattered across Valestia and the systems that run on top of it are hidden mysteries — things the game never tells you are there. Nobody hands you a quest for them. They are found, by playing: by going somewhere, trying something, noticing a detail, doing the unexpected.

Each one is one of a kind, and each pays off in its own way — a reward, a reveal, a surprise that exists nowhere else in the game. Some are small. Some are not.

One of them is a Pokemax that answers only a ritual — no shop, no spawn, one per account, never repeatable. It looks like this. Where and how are yours to find.

Your journal

!mystery (also !mistery) opens your mystery journal: what you have uncovered so far, and the unique things it earned you. It fills in as you find them.

No list here — on purpose. Half the point of a secret is not knowing it was there until you trip over it. This page will not tell you what to look for or where. Play, stay curious, and let Valestia surprise you.

If you find one, you will know. That is rather the idea.
