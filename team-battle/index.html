Demo rules and details

Jessie: two shots per normal attack, one resolved damage amount. Her special shoots both opponents, bypasses Parry and grants Focus.

Garrick — No One Else: once per battle, protects the whole team until round end. Intercepts single-target attacks, uses Parry and reduces incoming damage by 35%. A lethal hit leaves him at 1 HP and ends protection; he is not invulnerable.

Zoe — I pay attention: removes enemy Focus, prevents their criticals this round and gives +20% dodge to the living team. Throws a dagger at the chosen target.

Base rules without equipment, passives or consumables. Garrick stats come from his bot card; enemies are demo configurations. This is not connected multiplayer.
