Characters & mastery

Choose your fighting style, develop your character and make your branch choice count.

VALESTIA

Reviewed November 9, 2026Player reference · check the game for availability

Choose a character by the decisions you enjoy making. Open a card to compare all six stages and both branches. Each stage changes the previous values; percentages are not added together unless the rule says so.

6stages per character

The Guardian is free. Your first starter is free once per account; additional starters cost 45,000 OIL. Starter mastery upgrades total 180,000 OIL each. Jadewind is separate: 75,000 OIL to unlock and 270,000 OIL in upgrades. Only your selected character’s mastery applies in supported battles.

Your combat style

Try this sequence

At stage 1, two early successful hits build 2 charges; the next basic attack spends them.

Stage 1

Included with unlock

Anticipation · Hitting before your target’s turn builds 1 charge, up to 2. Your next basic attack spends both for 10% extra damage; that attack builds no charges.

Stage 2

10,000 OIL

Prepared thrust · An attack with 2 charges adds 12% damage.

Stage 3

30,000 OIL

Keep the advantage · A missed prepared attack preserves both charges.

Stage 4

25,000 OIL

Weapon technique · The primary attack of a weapon ability can also build and spend Anticipation.

Stage 5

80,000 OIL
Branch A

Decisive thrust: prepared damage increases to 15%.

Branch B

Tempo control: keeps 12%; a landed prepared attack drains 12 INI from the target.

Stage 6

35,000 OIL

Anticipation mastery ·

Branch A

prepared damage increases to 17%.

Branch B

a landed prepared attack drains 18 INI.

Limits and equipment interactions

Switching targets preserves charges. A prepared thrust works even if the rival acts first. Draining INI affects future rounds.

ExplorerComplete Rest → prepare evasion → face the next hit.＋

Your combat style

Try this sequence

Stage 1: successful Rest in round 1 prepares +10 dodge points. If unused, they expire at the end of round 2. This is a chance, not a guaranteed dodge.

Stage 1

Included with unlock

Prepared evasion · A successful REST prepares +10 dodge points against the next incoming attack. Expires at the end of the next round; charges do not stack.

Stage 2

10,000 OIL

Planned escape · Prepared evasion increases to +15 dodge points.

Stage 3

30,000 OIL

Second escape · Dodging the first prepared attempt preserves evasion for a second attempt, never a third.

Stage 4

25,000 OIL

Defensive reading · A Parry without a guard break prepares Evasion. Ending a round in Parry without taking HP damage also prepares it, even if nobody attacks you.

Stage 5

80,000 OIL
Branch A

Elusive: prepared evasion grants +20 dodge points.

Branch B

Safe retreat: keeps +15; a failed prepared dodge reduces that impact’s damage by 30.

Stage 6

35,000 OIL

Evasion mastery ·

Branch A

+25 prepared dodge points.

Branch B

reduce damage by 50 when that dodge fails.

Limits and equipment interactions

The global dodge cap still applies. Refreshing Evasion does not stack charges. From stage 4, a block without a guard break prepares Evasion immediately; ending a round in Parry without losing HP also prepares it for the next round, even if nobody attacks or shields absorb all damage. Healing after taking damage does not count as a damage-free round. A canceled Parry does not count. Safe retreat only reduces the impact that spent preparation, never indirect damage.

DuelistKeep attacking the same rival → build Pressure → strengthen later hits.＋

Your combat style

Try this sequence

Stage 1: hit A, then hit A again. The second hit benefits from the first Pressure point. Attacking B resets the chain.

Stage 1

Included with unlock

Pressure · Each landed attack against the same rival builds 1 point, up to 3. Each point adds 5% damage to later attacks. Changing targets or using a non-attack action resets Pressure.

Stage 2

10,000 OIL

Sustained duel · Store up to 4 Pressure points: up to 20% damage.

Stage 3

30,000 OIL

Measured pause · One REST between attacks preserves Pressure. A second consecutive non-attack action resets it.

Stage 4

25,000 OIL

Guard between strikes · That pause can also be a Parry without losing Pressure.

Stage 5

80,000 OIL
Branch A

Relentless: store up to 5 points, for up to 25% damage.

Branch B

Duel guard: Parry spends Pressure for 10 extra guard per point against that rival for the round.

Stage 6

35,000 OIL

Duel mastery ·

Branch A

the first hit against a new rival builds 2 points.

Branch B

15 extra guard per point, up to 60.

Limits and equipment interactions

Missing builds no Pressure. Points from a hit improve later attacks, never that same hit. Duel guard only protects against that rival and cannot stop attacks that bypass Parry.

Try this sequence

Stage 1: fully block in two different rounds to store 2 Firmness. A later guard break can spend both to reduce overflow damage by 40.

Stage 1

Included with unlock

Firmness · A full Parry block builds 1 Firmness per round, up to 2. A guard break spends the reserve to reduce overflow damage by 20 per point. Attacking or resting clears the reserve.

Stage 2

10,000 OIL

Defensive reserve · Store up to 3 Firmness: up to 60 guard-break damage reduction.

Stage 3

30,000 OIL

Measured defense · Spend only the points needed to absorb overflow damage, rounded up.

Stage 4

25,000 OIL

Persistent stance · Switching to attack or REST preserves up to 1 Firmness.

Stage 5

80,000 OIL
Branch A

Bastion: each Firmness reduces overflow damage by 30.

Branch B

Break stance: when choosing an attack, you may spend the reserve for 3% extra damage per point.

Stage 6

35,000 OIL

Firmness mastery ·

Branch A

reduce damage by 40 per point, up to 120.

Branch B

offensive spending adds 4% per point, up to 12%.

Limits and equipment interactions

Firmness only comes from full blocks. It cannot reduce attacks that bypass Parry. Branch B’s offensive spending is optional and is consumed even on a miss.

Overexertion · When choosing a basic attack, you may pay 8% of your max HP for 15% extra damage. Once every 2 rounds. The cost rounds up and cannot kill you.

10,000 OIL

Measured effort · Overexertion costs 7% of max HP.

Stage 3

30,000 OIL

Weapon surge · You may also empower the primary attack of a weapon ability.

25,000 OIL

Stage 5

80,000 OIL
Branch A

Drive: adds 21% damage and costs 8% of max HP.

Branch B

Restraint: adds 15% damage and costs 4% of max HP.

Stage 6

35,000 OIL

Overexertion mastery ·

Branch A

adds 23% damage for 7% of max HP.

Branch B

adds 18% damage for 4% of max HP.

The cost is shown before confirmation and paid on execution, even on a miss. You must have more HP than the cost. It triggers no shields, retaliation or damage-received effects.

ArcanistComplete Rest → store Resonance → spend it with Focus.＋

Your combat style

Try this sequence

Stage 1: successful Rest stores 2 Resonance. On an attack spending Focus with 100 base damage, Resonance adds 30 before defenses. Focus does not multiply that extra 30 again.

Stage 1

Included with unlock

Resonance · Each successful REST builds 2 Resonance, up to 2. The next attack spending Focus or Ultra Focus automatically spends the reserve: +15% of base damage per point, up to +30%.

Stage 2

10,000 OIL

Arcane reserve · Store up to 3 Resonance: up to +45% of base damage.

Stage 3

30,000 OIL

Defensive resonance · A full Parry block also builds 1 Resonance, at most once per round from this source.

Stage 4

25,000 OIL

Persistent echo · Missing preserves Resonance; Focus follows its normal consumption rules.

Stage 5

80,000 OIL
Branch A

Amplification: each Resonance adds 17% of base damage, up to 51%.

Branch B

Continuity: keeps 15%; spending at least 2 Resonance on an attack that does not miss returns 1 afterward.

Stage 6

35,000 OIL

Resonance mastery ·

Branch A

19% of base damage per point, up to 57%.

Branch B

17% per point, up to 51%, and keeps the 1 Resonance refund.

Limits and equipment interactions

No spending toggle: Resonance automatically applies to the first primary impact spending Focus. Extra damage uses base damage, is added after Focus and critical modifiers, and goes through normal defenses. A miss at stage 4 preserves the reserve and grants no additional refund. A full Parry block is not a miss. Continuity refunds at most 1 point per action and never preserves or creates Focus or Ultra Focus. Equipment restrictions still apply.

Included with unlock

Trail · A hit marks one rival until the end of the next round. Your later attacks against them face 10 fewer dodge points. Hitting another target moves the mark.

Stage 2

10,000 OIL

Sure trail · Your attacks against the marked target face 12 fewer dodge points.

Stage 3

30,000 OIL

Lasting trail · Trail lasts one additional round. A hit refreshes its duration.

Stage 4

25,000 OIL

Find the opening · Once per attack action (basic or weapon), the first hit that faces Parry from a target already carrying your Trail ignores 15% of their defense, up to 45 points. Hits without Parry and dodges do not spend the effect. Damage is not guaranteed if they can still block the entire hit.

Stage 5

80,000 OIL
Branch A

Huntress: adds 8% damage against the marked target at 35% HP or less.

Branch B

Pursuer: hitting the already marked target grants 6 INI, once per round.

35,000 OIL

Trail mastery ·

Branch A

the bonus works at 50% HP or less.

Branch B

initiative gain increases to 10 INI.

Limits and equipment interactions

Each tracker has one Trail. The hit creating the mark does not receive its benefits. Dodge cannot fall below zero; INI gains do not reorder the current round.

CustodianChoose Parry and an ally → share their final HP damage.＋

Your combat style

Try this sequence

Stage 1: if your protected ally would lose 100 HP after defenses, take 25 and they lose 75, while your round cap permits it. Your share can defeat you.

Stage 1

Included with unlock

Protection · When choosing Parry, choose an active ally. Take 25% of their final HP damage, up to 40 per round. When alone, reduce that share of damage overflowing your own Parry.

Stage 2

10,000 OIL

Sustained shelter · Protection’s limit increases to 60 per round.

Stage 3

30,000 OIL

Interpose · The protected share increases to 35%, capped at 60.

Stage 4

25,000 OIL

Strengthened vow · The limit increases to 80 per round.

Stage 5

80,000 OIL
Branch A

Personal guard: protect one ally with a limit of 100.

Branch B

Shared guard: you may protect two allies with a shared limit of 80.

Stage 6

35,000 OIL

Protection mastery ·

Branch A

limit of 120.

Branch B

shared limit of 100. Solo protection uses the same limit without doubling it.

Limits and equipment interactions

Damage is shared after the ally’s defenses and shields. Your share cannot be mitigated or redirected again; it can defeat you and end Protection. It triggers no further hits. Only one Protection per hit; Tag reserves do not participate.

WandererUse Attack, Parry and Rest → complete a sequence → earn its finisher.＋

Your combat style

Try this sequence

Stage 1: complete Attack → Parry → Rest. The closing Rest adds 35 healing only if it succeeds. Repeating Attack before the sequence is complete resets it.

Stage 1

Included with unlock

Rhythm · Complete Attack, Parry and REST in any order without repeating. The closing action gains +10% damage, +35 HP if REST succeeds, or +30 guard for that round.

Stage 2

10,000 OIL

Steady rhythm · Rewards increase to 12% damage, 45 HP or 40 guard.

Stage 3

30,000 OIL

Flexible rhythm · Repeat one action per sequence without resetting it; that repetition does not advance Rhythm.

Stage 4

25,000 OIL

Continuous path · The closing action also starts the next sequence.

Stage 5

80,000 OIL
Branch A

Offensive rhythm: closing with attack adds 16% damage; REST keeps 45 HP and Parry 40 guard.

Branch B

Disruptive rhythm: landing the Rhythm attack finisher removes the target’s Focus and adds 12% damage. Other finishers keep 45 HP or 40 guard.

Stage 6

35,000 OIL

Rhythm mastery ·

Branch A

the attack finisher adds 19% damage.

Branch B

the attack finisher adds 15% damage and removes Focus and Ultra Focus on a hit.

Limits and equipment interactions

Repeating an action resets the sequence, except for stage 3’s allowance. Canceled actions do not complete Rhythm; a missed attack spends its finisher. Interrupted REST does not heal. Finisher guard lasts only that round. Path B removes active and queued normal Focus only when the attack finisher’s primary hit connects, even if Parry blocks it. Stage 5 preserves Ultra Focus; stage 6 removes it. Secondary targets are unaffected. It replaces that path’s former special healing and guard bonuses.

AlchemistComplete Rest → create a dose → prepare support next round.＋

Your combat style

Try this sequence

Stage 1: Rest successfully, then next round use the 40 HP dose on an ally and choose your main action too. Only 2 doses may be created in the whole fight.

Stage 1

Included with unlock

Preparation · A successful REST creates 1 free dose: at most 2 created per battle and 1 stored. From the next round, use it to heal yourself or an active ally for 40 HP. It spends no inventory item and does not replace your main action.

Stage 2

10,000 OIL

Improved formula · Each dose can heal 60 HP. The total creation limit remains 2 per battle.

Stage 3

30,000 OIL

Formula reserve · Create up to 3 doses per battle and store 2. Each successful REST creates only one.

Stage 4

25,000 OIL

Protective formula · When using a dose, choose to heal 60 HP or grant an 80 shield until the end of that round. The total creation limit stays at 3 doses.

Stage 5

80,000 OIL
Branch A

Concentrated: create at most 4 doses per battle; each heals 80 HP or grants a 100 shield to one target.

Branch B

Shared: create at most 4 doses per battle; you may split the 60 HP or 80 shield 50/50 between yourself and an active ally, without duplicating it.

Stage 6

35,000 OIL

Preparation mastery · Both paths can create up to 5 doses per battle and store 2.

Branch A

100 HP or a 120 shield.

Branch B

a total of 80 HP or 100 shield; choose 50/50 or 75/25 and who receives the larger share. You may use the entire dose on one target.

Limits and equipment interactions

Doses are temporary class resources: they do not copy the equipped consumable, cost no OIL, and expire after combat. Interrupted REST creates no dose. At most one auxiliary use per round: a dose, the equipped consumable, or First Aid Kit; choose one. Doses do not amplify First Aid or receive Quartermaster’s consumable bonus. Healing restrictions apply; doses cannot revive. Dose shields do not stack with each other. Splitting conserves the total and stores no leftover portions. When alone, use the whole dose on yourself. Use the Class panel to queue a dose alongside your main action.

JadewindStop damage with Parry → store Current → spend it on attacks or shields.＋

Your combat style

Try this sequence

Parry must actually stop primary attack damage to generate Current. At stage 3, successful Rest can spend Current for a shield; interrupted Rest does neither.

Stage 1

Included with unlock

Receive the wind · Current is your energy reserve for this battle. Parry stores 30% of enemy damage stopped, up to 60 points: blocking 100 generates 30. Your next basic attack automatically discharges it as extra damage, subject to defenses. The extra cannot exceed 100% of that attack’s base damage, without Focus or criticals; keep any excess Current. At this stage, a dodge loses only the points committed to that discharge.

Stage 2

15,000 OIL

Deep breath · Parry now stores 40% of the damage stopped, and the reserve cap rises from 60 to 100. Blocking 100 generates 40 Current. If the enemy dodges your discharge, keep the entire reserve for another attempt. An attack blocked by Parry still counts as a hit and spends Current, even if it deals no HP damage.

Stage 3

45,000 OIL

Change the course · Choose Rest (REST) to automatically convert all your Current into a shield for yourself, at 1 shield point per point spent. With 80 Current, gain an 80-point shield. It absorbs damage; it does not restore HP. It lasts until the end of the next round unless depleted first. If REST is interrupted, gain no shield and spend no Current.

Stage 4

40,000 OIL

Continuous current · REST spends 75% of the reserve, rounded up. Attacks spend at most that 75%, also capped at 100% of the attack’s base damage. With 80 Current and 30 base damage, spend 30 and keep 50; if dodged, keep all 80. Offensive weapon actives can also discharge, only on their first primary hit. Additional hits do not repeat the bonus.

Stage 5

110,000 OIL
Branch A

Cutting gale: maximum reserve 140. A connected discharge prepares an automatic gust against the same enemy, after your action next round. Its base damage is 30% of Current spent, rounded down; it is not based on damage dealt. It can be dodged or blocked.

Branch B

Sheltering breeze: maximum reserve 140. The REST shield gains +25%: spending 60 produces 75 shield. It goes to you by default; use the Class panel to give the whole shield to a living, active ally instead. Save your choice and use REST; this is not an extra action.

Stage 6

60,000 OIL

Wind mastery ·

Branch A

maximum reserve 180; the gust rises to 50% of Current actually spent. With 160 reserve and at least 120 base damage, spend 120, keep 40 and prepare a 60-damage gust before defenses. With 50 base damage, spend 50, keep 110 and the gust is 25.

Branch B

maximum reserve 180; keeps the +25% shield bonus and allows a 50/50 or 75/25 split between you and an ally. Choose who gets the 75%. With 160, REST spends 120, keeps 40 and creates 150 total shield: 75/75 or 112/38. You may still give it all to one person.

Limits and equipment interactions

AVAILABILITY: experimental character, publicly locked. Stages improve the same mechanic; new percentages and caps replace earlier values rather than adding to them. BUILD CURRENT: only the portion of an enemy’s primary attack stopped by Parry counts, even if the remaining damage breaks your guard. Choosing Parry without being attacked grants nothing. Shields, self damage, allied attacks, reflections and secondary hits do not count. Generated points round down and cannot exceed your stage’s cap. Each mode keeps its Parry formula; dungeons use their own blocking rules. Start each battle with an empty reserve. DISCHARGE: your next eligible attack spends Current automatically. The extra is capped at 100% of that hit’s base damage, before Focus, criticals and other multipliers. Keep any excess in reserve. The gust uses only points actually spent. Focus and criticals do not multiply the extra; the attack or weapon’s defenses and damage rules still apply. A full Parry block counts as a hit and spends the committed points. A dodge preserves everything from stage 2. At stage 1 it loses committed points, capped by average base damage because no damage roll occurred; excess Current is kept. CREATE A SHIELD: requires a successful REST from stage 3. An interrupted REST spends no Current. Without a recipient choice, the shield goes to you. Branch B allows a living, active ally; if that ally becomes unavailable, conversion is canceled and you keep your reserve. The shield absorbs damage, does not heal or revive, and expires at the end of the next round. Overlapping mastery shields do not add their amounts together. Splits conserve the total, including rounding. BRANCH A GUST: automatically follows your action next round against the enemy hit by the discharge. Both fighters must remain alive and active. It has its own dodge check and respects Parry, mitigation and shields. It does not retrigger offensive effects or generate Current. If the target leaves or Jade is benched, the gust is canceled: it neither switches targets nor waits for Jade to return.
