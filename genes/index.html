Pokemax

Genes & Essence

Four hidden numbers rolled the moment you catch a Pokemax, and the only currency that can move them.

What a gene is

Every Pokemax rolls four genes at the moment you catch it — one per stat, each a hidden 0 to 15. They are permanent, they never change with level, and they are the difference between two Pokemax of the same species being identical or one being clearly better.

max HP = floor(base_hp × level ÷ 50) + level + 10 + geneatk / def / spd = floor(base × level ÷ 50) + 5 + gene

At level 50 a gene is worth a flat +15. That is enormous on a stat that lands around 60 — a perfect-gene Pokemax is roughly 25% stronger than a zero-gene one of the same species. This is the entire reason to keep hunting a species you already own.

See the gap for yourself in the Pokemax Lab: set a Pokemax to level 50 and slide the genes from 0 to 15.

Gene valueQuality
0–4Poor
5–8Average
9–11Good
12–13Excellent
14–15Exceptional

Genes are hidden until you scan them. A wild Pokemax looks identical whether it rolled 2s or 15s — the scan is how you find out before you spend an OILball.

Gene Essence

A separate currency, and it has exactly one source: releasing a Pokemax. The better its genes, the more it pays. You cannot release your last Pokemax.

Sum of the 4 genesQualityEssence paid
0–19Poor1
20–31Average2
32–43Good3
44–52Excellent4
53–60Exceptional5

What you can spend it on

OperationCostWhat it does
Scan Genes10 essenceReveal a wild Pokemax's genes mid-encounter, before you decide to spend an OILball on it.
Gene Training20 essenceRaise one gene by one point. 5 per Pokemax, ever.
Gene Reroll40 essenceReroll one gene at random. 2 per Pokemax, ever.
Gene Capsule75 essenceThe expensive one. Set a gene outright instead of gambling.

The economy is deliberately brutal. A Poor Pokemax pays 1 essence. Gene Training costs 20 and is capped at 5 per Pokemax, ever. So perfecting one Pokemax by hand means releasing twenty bad ones — and it still only buys 5 points out of a possible 60.

Catching a good roll will always beat grinding a bad one into shape. That is the point: the Safari is the game, not the workbench.

Advice

Scan before you throw. 10 essence is cheap next to a 3,000 OIL Prime OILball spent on a Poor roll.

Release your rejects. A Poor Pokemax sitting in your box is 1 essence you have not banked.

Spend Training on the stat that matters, not evenly. A sweeper wants SPD; a wall wants HP and DEF. Five points into the right stat beats five spread thin.
