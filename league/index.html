Pokemax

Pokemax League

The endgame past the ten Gyms: an Elite Four and a Champion, fought back to back, with a Legendary Pokemax and a permanent title on the line.

The Hall past the Gyms

The ten Gym medals were never the finish line — they are the key. Collect all ten and the Pokemax League opens: a sealed Hall where you face four Keepers — the Elite Four — and then the Champion, five authored battles back to back. It is the hardest Pokemax content in the game, and it only runs while Max has an edition open.

To challenge, you need all of: the League open, all 10 Gym Medals, a Party of exactly 3 Pokemax, no run already in progress, and you must not have already won the current edition. You get 3 attempts per day. Start it from the public Challenge the League panel — check your status any time with !league.

The five who wait

Each Keeper leads a different type, on purpose — no single team walks through all of them. Beat one and the next steps up.

The Champion twist: Morrigan Vex fights Dark, which nothing the Elite Four made you prepare for actually counters. Her ace is Umbryx — the game's first Legendary Pokemax. Clearing the League is a test of a collection, not of one favourite team. Study the type chart before you knock.

How a run works

The whole run happens in a private thread, one stage at a time — a Continue button launches each battle. Between stages you get a full reset: both sides return to full HP and full Move Uses, and you may rebuild your team of 3 from your entire collection, with no timer. Nothing that happens inside the Hall follows your Pokemax back out.

There are no checkpoints. Lose a single stage and the whole run is deleted — your next attempt starts again from Elite 1. Bring answers for all five types before you spend an attempt.

Beating the Champion

Win the Crown — once per edition — and you take home:

Every Champion, past and present, is listed with !hall_of_fame. When Max opens a new edition, the Hall resets its challenge and a fresh title is on the line — so being first has its own permanent place on the board.
