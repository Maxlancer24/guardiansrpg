Gear & Forge

Crafted gear boosts your stats in every mode. Two gear slots, saved per stat slot, enhanced up to +5.

Materials

Materials drop from finishing game modes — higher-stakes modes drop more — and from hunting regions. Four are your starter stock:

Starter materialCommon sources
Copper OreDuels, training, raids, dungeons
Silver OreContracts, missions, raids, dungeons
Linen FiberMissions, escort, contracts, dungeons
Cardboard ScrapLoot drops, triad, escort, FFA

Above those sit uncommon, rare and very rare tiers, plus 10 region signature materials that only drop from hunting one specific region, and two legendary weapon mats — Void-Tempered Core and Ascendant Ember — that no region drops at all.

Enhancement · +1 to +5

Equipped gear enhances from +1 to +5, making its bonus stronger each level, and it applies in every combat mode. Cost scales hard: the oil curve runs 20% → 35% → 60% → 100% → 150% of the item's base price per level, about 365% of the item's cost in total to reach +5.

The +4 and +5 wall is deliberate. Those two steps demand rare materials the earlier ones do not. Take your best piece to +3 before you even think about the last two.

Order of operations

stat slot → gear bonuses → enhancement → set bonus → combat

Everything stacks and applies automatically. My Stats shows the number that actually enters the fight.
