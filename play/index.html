Rest

Recover HP and gain Focus if not attacked. Attacks interrupt Rest and, if they land, deal ×1.5 damage before mitigation.

Special

Once per battle. Bypasses Parry and grants Focus, but can be dodged. Focus strengthens your next normal attack.
