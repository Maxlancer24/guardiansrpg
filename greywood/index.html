Greywood

Travel, hunts, regional materials, Pokemax and clan activities in the pale forest.

Greywood has its own access switch. If the game shows it as closed, travel and regional activities are unavailable. Opening access does not automatically open clan claiming. Check the current region panel for its status.

ArrivingHunts & materialsPokemaxGroup activitiesClan territory

How to get there

Travel from Starter Ruins or Ravennor. Greywood belongs to the mainland travel network: it is not an expedition island. Normal travel time, entry conditions and regional restrictions still apply. You do not need to have personally fought in the Liberation campaign to travel once access is enabled.

Hollowbark Grove is a forest of hollow trunks and resin. Stillwater Pools brings dark, still water and pale salt. Tannin Works and the Coast Road connect bark vats, medical stores and supply carts. These locations describe the region; choosing scenery does not guarantee a particular material.

Three enemy families, 21 names

Enemy stats scale with your level. Each family has seven names for bands 1–10, 11–20, 21–30, 31–40, 41–50, 51–60 and 61+. Later visual variants keep the final name.

Each category rolls independently. A successful category awards one material, one unit from that pool; the rare percentage is not the chance of each individual rare material. Greywood has no very-rare roll. Drop Level is the region’s investment level, not your character level.

Clan investment reaches Drop Level 10. Elites add 3 effective drop levels, up to 13 here: 24% rare and 16% Pale Amber before other applicable modifiers. Regional restrictions and existing boosts still apply.

Pale Amber is the only signature material. Tannin, Fiber and Salt are regional rares. All four come from Greywood hunts, not generic random rewards or the six missions listed below.

What are the materials for?

The four Greywood materials are used to enhance Quartermaster’s Seal beyond +3. The Seal itself is a campaign reward; these materials do not let you craft a new one.

These are only the Greywood ingredients. The upgrade also charges OIL and the other materials shown by the Forge. At base enhancement the Seal has +1 CON, +15 HP, +30% consumable healing/shields and 10 flat incoming attack reduction; each enhancement adds 4 percentage points to consumable amplification (+50% at +5).

The clan that controls Greywood can receive these combat missions through the territorial daily selection once the content and region are enabled. They are separate from cumulative clan objectives such as “win several battles.” Neither these missions nor the regional events award the Seal or Greywood’s four hunt materials.

Heart of Greywood

Greywood’s territorial passive starts at +2% maximum HP at Passive Level 1 to eligible clan members. It gains 1 percentage point per regional passive level, reaching +11% at level 10 before temporary regional modifiers. It is inactive while Greywood is closed or locked by a siege. It combines additively with Guardian Vitality when that PvE clan passive applies: at passive level 1, 2% + 15% = 17% over the same HP base, not 1.02 × 1.15. It is calculated when preparing combat and is not a repeatable heal.

The Liberation campaign is a separate cooperative event. Clearing a local event does not resolve that war, and the presence of a region in this guide does not announce its outcome. Follow the published campaign panel for fronts, objectives and rewards.
