Treasury, missions and forge

Your clan has shared daily objectives — win X of a mode, earn X Oil collectively — that pay the treasury in Oil and members in XP. The treasury funds passive upgrades and Clan Forge recipe unlocks, which gate six gear pieces and two weapons behind the clan rather than the player.

Clans also hold territory, which grants passive combat bonuses inside the zones they control.
