Clans

Permanent groups that grant collective passive bonuses, hold territory, and unlock their own forge recipes.

Greywood territory: Heart of Greywood starts at +2% maximum HP (regional Passive Level 1) when its territorial conditions apply. Access and clan claiming are separately enabled. Its three daily territorial combat missions use parties of 3, 5 and 8. Region details ↗

Treasury, missions and forge

Your clan has shared daily objectives — win X of a mode, earn X Oil collectively — that pay the treasury in Oil and members in XP. The treasury funds passive upgrades and Clan Forge recipe unlocks, which gate six gear pieces and two weapons behind the clan rather than the player.

Clans also hold territory, which grants passive combat bonuses according to the region they hold and its current state.
