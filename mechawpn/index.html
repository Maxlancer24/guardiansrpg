Mecha Weapons

Seven weapon families, each a different answer to the same question: how do you break four parts before they break yours.

The seven families

Each arm holds one. Values span Mk1 → Mk5.

Hits vs Power is the whole design. The Flamethrower throws six weak jets that scatter across the frame — it shreds several parts at once but bounces off armor. The Maul is one 199-damage swing that breaks anything it touches, if you can afford the 6 AP and it connects. Many-hit weapons cripple; single-hit weapons execute. Most pilots run one of each.

Match aim to weapon

A weapon's hit chance is its own accuracy plus the aim of the arm holding it. A Marksman Arm rescues the inaccurate heavy hitters — a Grenade Launcher on a Marksman Arm is a real threat; on a Guardian Arm it whiffs. A steady Guardian Arm instead keeps a reliable gun firing after the arm itself has been shot at.
