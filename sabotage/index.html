The guide

Clan Sabotage

Covert clan-versus-clan raids that plant a temporary curse on a rival's region — no conquest, no ownership change, just a knife in the dark that the owner and their allies get one window to stop.

VALESTIA

Raids, not conquest

Clan Sabotage is how you hurt a rival clan without trying to take their land. A sabotage never changes who owns a region — it slips in, plants a temporary effect, and slips out. It is the aggressive counterpart to Clan Diplomacy: diplomacy buys your way into someone's region, sabotage forces it.

Open the panel with !sabotage — pick the target region, then the effect. See every raid currently in play with !sabotages. Any clan member can call a Smuggle run; only leaders and sub-leaders can order the three damaging effects.

The four effects

A raid carries exactly one effect, and the effect sets how many raiders you bring:

EffectRaidersWhat it does
🚪 Smuggle1–3Your raiding party hunts that region free of the entry toll for the rest of the day — you force your own way in without a treaty.
📉 Drop Sabotage3−50% to the region's hunt drops for 12 hours. Everyone hunting there, owners included, gets half the loot.
🧬 Passive Sabotage3−50% to the region's territory passive for 12 hours — you halve the combat bonus the owners get from holding the land.
💰 Treasury Raid4Steal 5% of the enemy clan's treasury straight into yours, instantly, on the win. A clan can only be robbed once every 12 hours.

The defense window

Sabotage is not a sneak attack you cannot answer. The moment you launch, a defense window opens — its length is how long it takes your raiders to travel to the region (raid from next door and the window is short; raid from across Valestia and the defenders get real time to muster). A live countdown shows both sides exactly when the fighting starts.

The defending clan — and any clan it holds a Defense Alliance with — gets a lobby to put bodies on the wall. Whatever slots the defenders leave empty are filled by the region's garrison NPCs. Then a public thread opens in the region and the two sides fight it out as a team battle in the normal combat engine, with the garrison playing to win — no passive defending.

The garrison

A region's garrison is a clan upgrade, bought from the region's own upgrade panel and levelled from 0 to 10. It is your standing defense for when your members are offline — the higher the level, the harder the NPCs that fill your empty defense slots:

Garrison levelWhat the defenders get
0–3Baseline NPC defenders, scaled to the raiders' level.
4+The garrison NPCs start carrying combat passives.
8+An extra defender joins the line (up to four).
9That extra body becomes a Captain — a tougher, boss-tier NPC.
10The Captain is replaced by a doppelgänger of a clan member you designate — a frozen snapshot that fights with their real gear and their weapon's Active Skill.

The Lv.10 doppelgänger is the point of the whole track: a copy of your best-geared member standing guard around the clock, swinging their actual weapon and spending its Active exactly as they would.

Win, lose, and cooldowns

Raiders win → the effect lands: the debuff is planted, the smuggling waiver is granted, or the OIL is moved. Raiders lose → they come away wounded, locked out of starting or joining any sabotage for the rest of the day, and the defenders are paid a bounty for holding the line. A failed Smuggle is worse still: the whole party is barred from entering that region for the day — you were caught at the gate.

You cannot grind a single rival into the ground, either. The damaging effects sit on a cooldown against a given clan, and smuggling a given region is capped at once a day per raider. Sabotage is a scalpel, not a siege — for the fights that actually take and hold ground, see the large-scale modes.
