Building a Wanzer

Four parts — a body, two arms, legs — each in an archetype and a Mark, all under a weight budget the frame sets.

You assemble four parts, and each comes in an archetype and a Mark (Mk1–Mk5). The numbers below span Mk1 → Mk5; the shape of each archetype stays the same as it upgrades.

Legs are your evasion — Scout Legs dodge more than three times as often as Bastion Legs. It is also why blowing off the enemy's legs is such a strong opening: it turns their whole frame into an easy target.

Weight and evasion

Everything you bolt on has weight, and your body's output is the budget. Pack the frame past its limit and it slows down; pack it past 100% and it simply will not equip.

Weight vs outputEvasion
Up to 70%Full — no penalty
70% – 85%×0.80
85% – 100%×0.60
Over 100%Cannot equip

This is the build puzzle: a Bastion frame can carry the heaviest guns but barely dodges to begin with, so loading it further costs little. A Scout lives on evasion — hang a Maul off it and you throw away the one thing keeping it alive.

Build to the frame. Heavy frames want heavy guns and do not miss the evasion; light frames want to stay light and win the turn. Match your weapons to the body, not the other way around.
