Boss Raids

Learn each boss: the Centinel’s oath, Vareth’s breath and the Jade Regent’s two lives and Tempest.

VALESTIA

New: Severin’s illustrated encounter preview. His release status is shown separately from the established boss guides.

A raid asks three Guardians to coordinate their actions around one boss’s mechanics. Learn the warning, understand what happens next, and decide together who attacks, who protects and who recovers.

Before you enter

  1. Open Boss Raids in the player portal. Choose a published boss and difficulty, then create a group with teammates or use the available queue.
  2. Check the lobby’s entry requirements, cost, limits and rewards. Prepare your character, weapon, gear and consumables before the encounter starts.
  3. Agree on protection and recovery. Read the boss status every round; choosing Attack automatically is not a plan for a marked breath or an armed oath.

The examples below use the current base presets. A published raid may change its numbers, enabled mechanics and rewards; the lobby and battle warnings show the version you are playing.

01 / BOSS RAID

Break the oath. Survive the answer.

The armored guardian of The Last Watch punishes repeated attacks with Unbroken Oath. You must deal damage to win, but watch when your pressure arms his next strike.

Unbroken Oath, step by step

  1. Each direct Attack action that removes boss HP adds one charge. A multi-hit action counts once; an attack that is fully dodged or blocked adds nothing.
  2. At 3 charges, the oath is armed. It becomes usable from the next round, not immediately on the third hit.
  3. His next valid attack consumes the oath for +50% damage before defenses. The charge is spent even if you dodge or Parry that attack. The counter then starts over.

Example: three teammates attack

All three remove HP in round 1: the counter reaches 3/3. The empowered strike can happen from round 2. If the Centinel does not attack yet, the oath stays ready. Read the OATH READY warning before committing your next action.

What changes in phase II?

In the standard preset, ending a round alive at 40% HP or less triggers Enrage once. He recovers 10% of maximum HP and increases STR, AGI and CON by 10%, rounded up. This is the same life bar with a heal, so reaching the threshold is not a victory.

Your team’s plan

Track the oath together. When it is ready, protect vulnerable teammates and avoid counting on an unprotected Rest. Keep enough healing for Enrage; his increased AGI and CON also affect how safely you can trade attacks.

Ultimate and the golden armor

The current Ultimate preset uses the gilded appearance: golden armor and a crimson cape. Its Enrage threshold remains 40%, but the heal is 5% and the attribute increase is 15%. Unbroken Oath remains 3 charges and +50%. The Oathforged is the title configured for this preset; confirm the published raid’s rewards before entering.

02 / BOSS RAID

One marked ally. Three ways to weaken the flame.

Vareth’s signature mechanic is Banked Flame: a warning that announces who will receive Ashen Breath. The marked player needs protection; the whole group can reduce the breath’s bonus by damaging the dragon.

The charge → breath → recovery cycle

  1. With the base interval of 3 rounds, Vareth starts charging in round 2. He marks the living Guardian with the most current HP at that moment and announces the breath for round 3.
  2. The breath begins at +80% damage. While it is charging, each different Guardian who lands an Attack that removes boss HP reduces this bonus by 20 percentage points, once per charge.
  3. The announced breath is an extra attack after his normal actions. It keeps the marked target; ordinary attacks can still threaten other players.
  4. After the breath, the charge resets. In the base cycle, the next warnings arrive in rounds 5, 8… and the breaths in rounds 6, 9… Follow the live countdown if the interval differs.

Three hits from the same Guardian only count as one contribution: the bonus stays at +60%. A multi-hit weapon does not let one player provide all three reductions. You weaken the bonus; you do not cancel the underlying attack.

Phase II: Ancient Flame

In the base preset, reaching the end-of-round Enrage threshold of 40% HP increases STR, AGI and CON by 12%, rounded up, with no phase-transition heal. The flame mechanic remains active. Vareth does not use the Centinel’s Unbroken Oath.

Your team’s plan

During the charge, spread damaging Attacks across different teammates when it is safe. Before the announced breath, help the marked player with suitable protection and recovery. Parry, dodge, shields and mitigation still matter. If the marked player falls before the breath, it dissipates instead of silently jumping to another target.

03 / BOSS RAID

Break the seal. Endure the tempest.

This encounter has two separate lives. Defeating The Sealed Blade releases Tempest Unbound. Save enough resources to fight the second phase and watch its own round counter.

Phase I → phase II: what happens at zero HP?

  1. Reduce The Sealed Blade to zero HP. This ends the current round: remaining actions are cancelled. Excess damage does not carry into the new life.
  2. Tempest Unbound begins with a new, full HP pool. Everyone chooses new actions for the next round. Do not rely on a queued heal or another attack from the interrupted round.
  3. The base second phase has 20% more maximum HP, approximately 15% more STR after rounding, +3 AGI and the same CON as phase I. Normal, Heroic and Legendary scale the starting attributes.

Tempest: every third complete round of phase II

Count from the start of phase II: Tempest is due in its rounds 3, 6, 9…. It replaces one of the Regent’s normal actions. Each strike uses 75% of a normal attack’s damage before defenses in the current preset: this means 0.75×, not an additional +75%.

Your team’s plan

Enter phase II with HP and consumables in reserve. Plan recovery and shields before its third round, then repeat every three rounds. Keeping teammates alive spreads Tempest’s pressure. Do not mistake the first empty life bar for the end of the raid.

Crimson Sovereign

Crimson Sovereign is the Regent’s crimson appearance, with its own palace scenery and phase names: The Crimson Seal and Crimson Tempest. Check the published version for its attributes and rewards; appearance alone does not set difficulty.

Reward guide: Heir of the Crimson Tempest

When equipped, this title grants +1 STR and +1 AGI. Its Crimson Echo releases wind pulses on every third Attack action that removes HP: 15% of base damage against up to three different enemies. Multi-hit actions count once. A fully blocked attack neither advances nor resets the counter; a guard break counts only when HP is lost.

Pulses can be dodged or blocked, respect mitigation and shields, and do not trigger a Parry counterattack. Focus, criticals and offensive multipliers do not amplify them. The title must be offered by the published encounter and equipped to apply its effect.

Two lives. One marked target.

The first life is The Last Hunter; defeating it begins The Pale Procession with its own HP pool. In phase II, Hunter’s Verdict adds an extra shot every three rounds. Read the warning and countdown on the battle panel.

  1. 01
    Warning: one round before the shot, Severin marks the living Guardian with the most current HP. It is current HP, not maximum HP.
  2. 02
    Prepare: coordinate protection and recovery for that Guardian. The mark stays on the announced target; it does not jump to a teammate because HP rankings change.
  3. 03
    Shot: the extra attack uses 200% of normal attack damage before defenses, without Focus, Ultra Focus or critical multipliers. If the marked target becomes invalid, the shot cancels instead of silently retargeting.
  4. 04
    Following round: if the shot removes HP and the Guardian survives, Hunter’s Curse halves STR, AGI and CON for that next round. Current and maximum HP do not change. Do not plan your next attack as if your attributes were untouched.
