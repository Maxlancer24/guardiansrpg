What It Looks Like

The actual combat screens, as the bot draws them in Discord. Every layout below is reproduced from the code that builds it — the same HP bars, stat lines and turn prompts you will see live.

There is no mystery to what a fight looks like. The bot draws a text embed each round with everyone's HP, stats and chosen action, then waits for you to press a button. Below is every screen in the game, drawn exactly as the code builds it.

The bars are literal: █ is HP you have, ░ is HP you have lost. ✅ next to a name means that fighter has locked their action this round; ⏳ means the round is still waiting on them.

Team battles

One engine draws the screen for almost every group fight — Duels, Team & Tag, Triad, Free-For-All, Escort, Horde, Mission, Vanguard, War and Boss Raids. What changes between them is the footer and a couple of extra fields, shown further down. This is the core layout:

The 🟦/🟥 headers are the team names. Item: shows the consumable you have queued for the turn, or Used once spent. The ⚡🩸 after the Pathfinder are its NPC passives — Momentum and Lifesteal — shown as icons so you know what it does before it does it.

1v1 Duel

Same engine, two fighters, and both of you pick at the same time — the choices reveal together. The only tell is the footer: no target to select, so it just says to choose carefully.

🔥 FOCUS next to Max means his next attack is a guaranteed ×2 — the flag turns into 🌟 ULTRA FOCUS once it is unreducible. Rival still shows ⏳: the round is waiting on their pick.

Tag Duel

Tag fights split the screen into who is in and who is on the bench. The active lane shows full stats; benched fighters are one compact line each, and a fainted one is struck through.

Switching a benched fighter in costs your turn, so the bench view is deliberately compact — you are reading it to decide who to rotate, not to fight from it.

Battle Royale

The training free-for-all tracks a temporary match level and in-match XP that climb as you act — its screen shows both instead of gear consumables.

🔥 Round Active - Choose Action

The arena narrows. Only one walks out.

Max (Match Lvl 3) STR:32 AGI:13 CON:12[█████████░░░] 160/200 HP | ⚡ XP: 40/100

Contender (Match Lvl 2) STR:18 AGI:15 CON:10[██████░░░░░░] 90/180 HP | ⚡ XP: 25/100

💤 Rest: Heal for +15 HP +15 XP ⏰ 2 Minutes to choose.

Attacking and killing feed the XP bar; hit 100 and your match level ticks up for a small mid-fight boost. It resets when the match ends — only the winner keeps global XP.

Horde & the shared-engine extras

The modes that field NPC allies or waves add fields onto the team screen. Horde shows both an Ally Plan (what the NPCs intend this round) and Horde Progress (the wave counter). Escort and Mission add the same Ally Plan plus a 🛡️ Protect NPC action.

These extra fields are the only real difference between the group modes. Underneath, it is the same combatant rows as the team screen at the top.

The Eternal Abyss

The dungeon draws its own screen: a score and gold header, the run's class icons on each player, and dungeon-only status like momentum stacks and stun.
