Competitive badges

All 37 competitive badges: criteria, bonuses, eligibility and preferences.

VALESTIA

Reviewed November 9, 2026Player reference · check the game for availability

37badge categories
1owner per badge
1badge per player

Badges are competitive distinctions. Leading a metric does not permanently unlock a collectible for everyone: ownership is reassigned according to eligible rankings and player preferences. Their bonuses apply while you hold the badge.

Eligibility and choosing a preference

Regular candidates need a positive score and at least one Guardians combat in the last 14 days. The activity window does not reset lifetime metrics. Story accounts do not compete. If the game offers several eligible choices, select a preference; a successful change locks that preference for 3 days. This cooldown does not guarantee ownership for three days.

You still need to qualify for the selected badge. Apex Vanguard only belongs to the highest-ranked eligible player; if that player obtains a preferred different badge, Apex can remain vacant. Check the game for current owners and available choices.
