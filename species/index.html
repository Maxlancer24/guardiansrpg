VALESTIA

Reviewed November 9, 2026Player reference · check the game for availability

Explore 141 ordinary species in 47 three-stage families. Harrowmere adds four families. Hidden and unique discoveries are excluded; Primordial is an awakening of an existing species, not another species.

Evolution thresholds are levels 16 and 36. The normal cap is 50; a supported Primordial can train to 60 after awakening. The numbers below are base stats, not a trained partner’s final stats.
