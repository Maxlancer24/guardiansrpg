129 catalogued species in 43 three-stage families, including Greywood. Special discoveries are separate.

Explore 129 ordinary species in 43 evolution families: 31 mainland lines and 12 expedition lines. This catalog excludes hidden and special-discovery species. Evolution thresholds are level 16 and 36; the normal level cap is 50.
