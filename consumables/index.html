Consumables

How to equip and use consumables, with the current base effects of all 18 items.

VALESTIA

Equip a consumable before joining. In battle, open Use Consumable, choose a target if requested, and confirm. Your choice is queued for the round; the inventory item is spent when the effect resolves. Then choose your main action too. A potion alone does not complete your turn.

In the standard shared combat system, the equipped inventory consumable has one use per combat. Multiple bottles in your bag do not give you a potion every round. Some modes have separate rules. Free action means it does not replace Attack, Parry, Rest or your weapon active; it does not mean unlimited items.

Before resolution begins, the confirmation panel lets you cancel the queued consumable. Opening a menu or cancelling is not the same as using the item. The controls stop accepting changes once the round is resolving.

Base values before mastery and gear modifiers. Healing cannot exceed missing HP unless the item explicitly raises maximum HP. Check availability, prices and mode restrictions in the game.

One main action. A separate support choice.

Reviewed November 9, 2026Player reference · check the game for availability

Choose Attack, Parry, Rest or a weapon active as your main action. If you use a consumable, confirm its target as well. Using a potion does not submit your main action. The item is spent when its effect resolves.

Alchemist: choose one auxiliary use per round. A dose, your equipped consumable or First Aid Kit. You cannot combine all three. Doses do not spend inventory and do not receive Quartermaster’s consumable amplification.
