The world

Lore

What the scroll says, what is under Aldenmoor, and what is happening to the water in Stormbay harbour.

Azroth

A malign being, sealed. The seal is failing. The three locations named in the scroll all connect to the original seal, and the seal needs three pieces to be repaired — one in each place.

The Scroll

Written in a dead script and co-authored by Gorim and the Varn family. It describes the road to three places: Aldenmoor (ruins, north-west), Veldran (a city in the mountains) and Mirefall (a swamp). Elric spent thirty years on the script before he ever met you.

The Seal and the Varns

The Varns built the mechanism. Maren Varn — the last of them, hidden for thirty years — had the key, and gave it to you. The old maps of the tunnels have intentional errors: the original builders did not trust outsiders.

The dark circle

What the Harbor Knows

Thirty feet across. The water inside it is completely still. Absolutely nobody on the docks looks at it.

It appeared in Stormbay harbour, and then again at the cove where the Grey Maren made landfall. It is Azroth, leaking through.

The Hollow

Creatures born from Azroth's seepage into Aldenmoor. Distorted humanoid shapes with no eyes, that dissolve into smoke when they die. They come up from the old underground entrances. They were the first thing in this story that was not human.

The Archive

Below Aldenmoor there is something older than Aldenmoor. The refugees dug a granary foundation and found a shaft; three workers went down and one came back wrong.

The cast

The Guardian
You
Chosen by prophecy — Gorim's word, not yours. You carry the scroll and Maren's key.
Jessie
Mercenary · Gunslinger
Hard on the outside, deliberately: she keeps the reputation and the distance as armour. Underneath she is loyal and kind and almost never shows it. Found as a baby in the Greywood; she has never known who her parents were. She goes quiet when anyone says Veldran.
Elric
The Scribe · Scholar
Shy, quiet, very smart. Reads dead languages. Wants to belong with the group and feels like an academic among people of action. When he does speak he is usually right.
Cara
Broker
Pawnshop on the South Docks, crossbow always loaded, knows everything and everyone. She and Jessie have a history neither of them will explain. She stayed in Stormbay.
Valdris
The antagonist
Buys loyalties. Reached Stormbay four days ahead of you and has never once been behind since. He issued arrest warrants through the city's own legal channels, which tells you what he owns.
Gorim the Old
Keeper of the Scroll
Guarded it for years and co-wrote it. He knew who you were before you did. He has not appeared in person since.
Maren Varn
The last Varn
Thirty years hidden. Gave you the key. Described a couple who went to Aldenmoor thirty years ago: she had hands like a fighter and eyes that didn't trust easily; he talked too much.
Garrick
Leader of the Aldenmoor refugees
Strong, charismatic, gives everything for his people, still optimistic despite the losses. He lost his brother on the second failed attempt to reach the underground stores.
Zoe
Jessie's childhood friend
Cheerful, fearless and forever getting into trouble. Grew up in Veldran with Jessie and knows the city — and its council receptions — inside out. Loves Jessie unconditionally; Jessie loves her back and is quietly terrified of the day she runs at something that does not work out.
The Frogst Brothers
Barrel and Hammer
Korn Army, but they came for you personally the night of the escape. Jessie faced both of them alone.
