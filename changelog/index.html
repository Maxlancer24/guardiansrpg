What's New

Recent additions to The Guardians and this compendium, newest first.

This page tracks the bigger additions to the game and to this site. The compendium itself is read from the live bot, so day-to-day tuning (prices, drop rates, caps) can change without a note here.

2026-08-10 · New expedition: The Hollowstar Crater. A fallen-star crater driven by a community Starfall event instead of tides, with 12 new Pokemax (now 108 catchable), the Craterglass Bracer gear, the Meteor Anchor weapon, Starseal currency, and the Starwarden's Trial for the unique Cosmoryx.

2026-08-08 · New Main Story mission — "Soran Kael": the Chapter 3 finale. Kael comes alone to offer Jessie the alliance he once offered her mother; she refuses and fights him down, and he warns that whoever Valdris sends next will not open with an offer. The team leaves Veldran for Mirefall — Chapter Three is complete.

2026-08-07 · New Main Story mission — "The West Gate" (Chapter 3): the escape from Veldran. Bren stays behind to take the inquiry, Zoe defies Jessie to join the fight, and the west-gate break-out fails — the gate shuts and Soran Kael steps into the yard alone to meet Jessie face to face.

2026-08-06 · New Main Story mission — "What Bren Knew" (Chapter 3): the Veldran arc's reveal. Bren finally tells Jessie the truth about her parents — seal-hunters killed by Valdris — and the real prize the story has been circling: the hidden activation, and a third site called Mirefall.

2026-08-05 · New on the Sunken Fang Archipelago: The Tidekeeper's Trial. Beat Tidekeeper Neris's three Pokemax (one-time victory, unlimited retries) to earn Nautyrant — Tidebound, a unique Water all-rounder with the tide-reactive Tidal Covenant passive, plus Fang Doubloons and OIL.

2026-08-05 · New Main Story mission — "The Cistern Wall" (Chapter 3): the escape from the sealed district. Bren spends the one favor eleven years of council standing bought him to open a signed door, you hold the alley against a Valdris hunt team, and — safe at last — Bren finally starts to answer why Valdris knows Jessie's face.

2026-08-04 · New Main Story mission — "The Alarm" (Chapter 3): the escape. Soran Kael weighs the replica, knows it is false, and calmly seals the district — because Valdris once described Jessie to him. You fight a Valdris strike squad in the plaza and run into the Veldran night with the alarm ringing.

2026-08-03 · New Main Story mission — "The Exchange" (Chapter 3): the auction heist reaches its climax. You and Zoe make the relic swap while Jessie holds the table, a returning guard and the Chamber Warden crash the chamber, and Soran Kael signs for a prize that is not the one he paid for.

2026-08-02 · The Sunken Fang expedition Pokemax — Driftfin → Galeray → Stormanta, Shellwisp → Lanternclaw → Abysshell, Coralisk → Reeflisk → Leviatide and Sprigrove → Mangrover → Tidegrove — now show up in the Pokemax Lab and on Species. The expedition's exclusive gear and weapon are explained with full Base → +5 numbers.

2026-08-01 · The entire compendium is now available in Spanish — use the EN / ES buttons in the sidebar to switch at any time.

2026-08-01 · Added an on-site search box (find any gear, weapon, Pokemax, move or region by name), a Getting Started FAQ, and a contact address.

2026-07-31 · New Expeditions section: timed sea voyages from Ravennor, starting with The Sunken Fang Archipelago — six-hour tides, Fang Doubloons, and tide-only Pokemax.

2026-07 · Beginner combat and Pokemax tutorials, chapter climax art across the Main Story, and clearer consumable and Pokemax details.
