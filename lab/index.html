How to read it

The stat bars compare this Pokemax against the strongest final-stage Pokemax in the game for that stat, so a full bar means nothing else beats it. The grey ghost behind each bar is the same Pokemax with zero genes — the gap is what your roll is worth.

Drag the level past 16 and 36 and the Pokemax evolves in front of you, base stats and all. That is exactly what happens in a battle.

Moves known are the last four learned at or below the current level — the same rule the bot uses. Raise the level and watch an early move fall off the list. The Full learnset below it lists every move the Pokemax can learn and the level it comes at, with power, PP and effect — dimmed rows are ones it has not reached yet.
