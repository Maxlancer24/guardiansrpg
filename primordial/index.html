Pokemax progression

Primordial awakening

How to earn Resonance, awaken a supported final evolution and raise its level cap to 60.

A Primordial is your existing Pokemax awakened: it keeps its species and gains that species’ Primordial passive, plus a level cap of 60. It does not instantly jump from 50 to 60.

1. Choose an eligible partner

Use a supported final evolution at exactly level 50 and gather 10 Resonance for that exact species. Finish any battle, challenge or trade first; recover a captured partner from Team Comet and remove it from Gym defense if necessary. The awakening panel checks your current eligibility.

2. Earn Resonance by releasing compatible duplicates

A released Pokemax must be a supported species, not already Primordial, never traded, and have at least 45 total genes. Add HP + ATK + DEF + SPD (each ranges from 0 to 15). The gene threshold is for earning Resonance from release; it is not an extra awakening requirement for your chosen partner.

Resonance belongs to the exact supported final species. Releasing an earlier evolution does not give the final evolution’s Resonance. Release removes that Pokemax from your collection: review the confirmation carefully.

3. Train and use its passive

After awakening, train toward level 60. Normal stat growth continues; the passive is a separate combat effect. Greywood’s Oakenfort, Foragrove and Brackenmaw are not currently in the awakening catalog. Availability follows the game’s current feature setting.
