Your home base: the Kiosk

Look for the persistent Valestia RPG panels in Discord. The Kiosk brings together your profile and the main game systems. Most actions use buttons; you do not need to memorize a list of commands.

A simple first session

  1. Create your profile through the Kiosk.
  2. Open Characters & Mastery. Keep the Guardian or choose your free starter.
  3. Check your equipment and consumables before joining a hunt.
  4. Read the battle controls. Attack deals damage, Parry anticipates a hit, and a successful Rest prepares Focus.
  5. After the fight, check your rewards and choose your next activity.

Questions before you begin

What is The Guardians?

A fantasy RPG set in Valestia, with turn-based battles, a story campaign, character mastery, clans, Pokemax and mechas. Discord hosts the community and game panels; the player portal and supported web battles connect to the same game.

Do I need Discord?

You can explore this guide and try the demo without signing in. For your persistent character, join the Discord, create your profile through the Kiosk and sign in to the portal with that same Discord account.

Does the demo save my progress?

The public demo is a separate practice experience. It does not grant items, OIL or progression to your game account.

Is my first character free?

The Guardian is always free. Your first choice among the 11 starter characters is free once per account. Additional starters cost 45,000 OIL each. Check the confirmation shown in the game before a purchase.

Where do I find events and rewards?

Check the current Discord panels. Event availability, entry requirements and rewards depend on the server configuration; this guide is a reference, not a live event feed.
