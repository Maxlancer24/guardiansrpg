Getting Started

There is no registration. Your account is created the first time you make a profile from the Kiosk, or the first time you join a game — whichever happens first.

The Guardians is a Discord RPG built and run by Max — the combat engine, the world of Valestia, the 85 story and side missions, all of it.

Everything on this site describes the bot as it actually runs on the server the bot lives in. Reward amounts, drop rates, daily caps, prices and which panels are up are set by Max, and the defaults listed here are what he starts from.

The Kiosk

The bot uses the ! prefix for typed commands, but most of the game is panel-driven: Max places persistent button panels in the channels and you play by clicking them. The most important one is the Kiosk — your personal hub.

Your first hour

1 — Make a profile. Open the Kiosk, create your character, and spend your points. You start with 10.

2 — Set up two builds. It is free. Put an attacker in slot 1 and a tank in slot 2. Apply the right one before a fight.

3 — Fight something. !duel @player for a duel, !hunt to farm a region, or whatever panel is up on the server.

4 — Craft one starter piece. A Strength Ring or Cardboard Boots costs 4,000 OIL and changes every fight you take afterwards.

Open My Stats before any fight. It shows your build after gear and enhancement are applied. It is the only number that matters, and it is not the number in your stat slot.

Stat slots and builds

You have 3 stat slots. Each one stores a full build: a STR / AGI / CON spread and two gear pieces. Gear stacks on top of the slot — a slot with STR 8 plus a Strength Ring is STR 9 in combat.

A working set: slot 1 STR/AGI for duels and FFA · slot 2 CON parry tank for Horde, Missions and sieges · slot 3 high-AGI dodge build.

Frequently asked questions

What is The Guardians?
A role-playing game that runs entirely inside Discord: turn-based combat, craftable gear and weapons, a story campaign, a full monster-catcher (Pokemax), mechs and more — all played with chat commands.

Is it free to play?
Yes. The Guardians is a Discord bot game; this website is a free player compendium read from the live bot.

How do I start?
Join the Discord server where the bot lives and make your first profile from the Kiosk — your account is created the moment you make a profile or join your first game. The Getting Started commands on this page walk you through it.

What are Pokemax?
A complete monster-catcher living inside the RPG: catch 96 species across Valestia, raise them to level 50 through two evolutions, and battle 3-versus-3 — with its own type chart, moves and genes.

Who makes and runs the game?
The Guardians is built and run by Max on his Discord server. Reward amounts, drop rates, prices and which features are live are all set by Max; this site simply reflects the live bot.

How do I get in touch?
